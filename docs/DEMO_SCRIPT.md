# Walkthrough Video Script: Daily Hype Battle (Under 60 Seconds)

A fast-paced, high-energy walkthrough demonstrating the full gameplay loop to evaluators.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Action Script

| Time | Screen Action | Voiceover / Text Overlay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:05** | Start on the Reddit feed showing the Daily Hype Battle post. Show the blinking `🟢 Today’s board is open` badge. | *"Welcome to Daily Hype Battle! The Reddit-native daily game where you pick today’s meme and launch tomorrow’s contender."* |
| **0:05 - 0:15** | Click **"Enter the Arena 🔥"** to expand the card into the full-screen steppers playfield. Show the progress bar at 0/100. | *"Enter the Arena. Every day, you get 100 fictional Hype Points to support the community’s meme contenders."* |
| **0:15 - 0:25** | Rapidly click `+` and `-` to distribute points (e.g. 50 on Frog Vibes, 30 on Dumpster Fire, 20 on Chaos Duck). Watch the card glow purple. Hit exactly 100 points, watch the Lock button bounce. Click **"Ready to lock your hype 🔒"**! | *"Spread your points across the contenders. Once you hit exactly 100 points, lock your choices."* |
| **0:25 - 0:35** | Show the Locked screen ticket receipt. Scroll down to show the `"LOCKED"` stamp and the waiting instructions. Click **"Nominate Tomorrow's Meme 🚀"**. | *"Your choices are sealed as a verified ticket receipt. While waiting for the round to settle, open the Meme Launchpad."* |
| **0:35 - 0:45** | Nominate a meme by clicking emoji preset chips, type name, and click submit. Settle the round using the **"Reveal Results 🏆"** control under the Round Controls at the bottom. | *"Submit your tomorrow's candidate or support community nominees. Let's settle the round to see who won!"* |
| **0:45 - 0:55** | Show the Results screen: champion reveal glow, stats grid, leaderboards, tomorrow's board preview watchlist, and copy the recap with **"Copy Recap"**. | *"Boom! Dumpster Fire is crowned champion! Copy your recap, check streak records, badges, and tomorrow's lineup preview!"* |
| **0:55 - 1:00** | Zoom in on the footer disclaimer. Close script. | *"Daily Hype Battle. Fictional points only. Play today, nominate tomorrow!"* |

---

## 💡 Recording Tips
1. **Speed**: Run the script quickly. Use a browser auto-clicker or click fast to avoid wasting seconds on allocations.
2. **Branding**: Make sure the purple glows, receipt ticket, and neon badges are fully visible.
3. **Contrast**: Capture in Dark Mode if possible, as the arcade colors pop beautifully against dark themes.
